### Overview

AI researcher and engineer focused on building reliable, reproducible machine learning systems from theoretical research to high-throughput production.

My work centers around **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **multimodal vision-language models**, **quantum machine learning**, and **end-to-end MLOps**. I enjoy converting rigorous ideas from research literature into robust architectures, reproducible benchmarks, and scalable services.

### Research & Engineering Focus

- **RAG & Information Retrieval:** Evaluating advanced retrieval architectures, re-ranking, and modular chunking paradigms.
- **Vision-Language Alignment:** Probing cross-modal representation spaces and fine-tuning lightweight multimodal models.
- **Quantum-Classical Hybrids:** Designing parameter-efficient quantum neural networks and hybrid variational circuits.
- **MLOps Reliability:** Hardening model serving latencies, automated retraining pipelines, and reproducible experiment tracking.
